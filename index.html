Before participating

Trust & eligibility first.

These safeguards are part of the project's original requirements and should remain visible.

Eligibility matters

  • The supplied project material states that Zulti's terms require users to be 18 or older.
  • Availability may depend on jurisdiction.
  • Do not use a VPN or another method to bypass geographic restrictions.
  • Do not create multiple accounts or engage in account farming.

Protect your account

  • Never give another person your password or OTP.
  • Never share seed phrases or private wallet credentials.
  • Be cautious of people claiming to guarantee rewards or profits.
  • Check Zulti's current official terms before participating.
